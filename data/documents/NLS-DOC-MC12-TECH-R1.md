## Purpose

This note explains which tubes and rotors can be used together, and how to balance a load. It applies to both the Standard and the Plus unless stated.

## Compatible tubes

- 12-place rotor (both versions): 1.5 ml and 2.0 ml snap-cap tubes, including Northfield microcentrifuge tubes 1.5 ml (rated to 20,000 ×g).
- 8-place PCR strip rotor (Plus only): 0.2 ml tube strips. This rotor is not available for the Standard.
- Tubes must be rated for at least the maximum RCF of your version: 14,200 ×g (Standard) or 18,900 ×g (Plus).

## Balancing

1. Load tubes in opposite positions.
2. For an odd number of tubes, add a balance tube of the same type with the same volume of water.
3. Check that liquid volumes in opposite tubes differ by no more than 0.1 ml.

## Speed and RCF

RCF depends on speed and rotor radius: RCF = 1.118 × 10^-5 × radius (cm) × rpm². For the 12-place rotor (7.5 cm) this gives 1,300 ×g at 4,000 rpm, 5,400 ×g at 8,000 rpm and 14,200 ×g at 13,000 rpm.

## Not covered

Use with tubes larger than 2.0 ml, or with adapters not supplied by Northfield, is not covered by this note.
