## Purpose

This note explains the temperature performance of the HB-2 and how to use the blocks correctly. It applies to both versions.

## Temperature uniformity

Temperature uniformity across a block is ±0.5 °C at 37 °C. Allow the block to stabilise for at least 10 minutes after reaching the set point before starting a timed run.

## Heat-up

Heat-up to 100 °C takes about 20 minutes (Single block) or about 22 minutes with both blocks of the Dual block heating together.

## Using the blocks

- Each block holds 20 × 1.5 ml tubes. Seat tubes fully so they touch the bottom of the wells.
- Change blocks only when the heater is switched off and cool to the touch; no tools are needed.
- Do not use a damaged block.
- Dual block: each block has its own temperature control and timer.

## Limits

The maximum temperature is 150 °C. The over-temperature cut-out operates at 165 °C and is a safety device, not an operating setting.
