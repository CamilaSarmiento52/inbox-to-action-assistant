## Purpose

This note explains the operating modes of the VM-10 and which tubes can be mixed. It applies to both versions unless stated.

## Operating modes

- Basic: continuous operation only, using the on/off switch, at a fixed 2,800 rpm.
- Variable speed: set the speed between 200 and 3,000 rpm with the dial. Touch mode runs while the tube presses the cup head; continuous mode runs until switched off.

## Tube compatibility

The cup head accommodates tubes up to 15 ml. Mixing 50 ml tubes is not recommended.

## Duty cycle

Run continuously for no more than 30 minutes, then rest the unit for 10 minutes.

## Good practice

- Hold the tube firmly and avoid overfilling; fill tubes to no more than two-thirds.
- Start at a low speed and increase gradually (Variable speed only).
