## Purpose

This note explains which pipettes the Northfield filter pipette tips 200 µl fit.

## Fit

The tips are universal-fit tips for single-channel pipettes of 20 to 200 µl with a standard conical tip cone. Press the tip on firmly and check that it seals before use.

## Not covered

The tips are not intended for multichannel pipettes with a non-standard cone, or for pipettes outside the 20 to 200 µl range. Northfield does not list individual pipette models; test the fit on your own pipette before routine use.

## Handling

- Keep tips in the rack until use to preserve sterility.
- Close the box lid after use.
