## 1. Introduction

The VM-10 is a benchtop vortex mixer with a cup head. It is sold in two versions: the Basic (fixed speed) and the Variable speed (adjustable speed with touch and continuous modes).

Differences between the versions:

|  | Basic | Variable speed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed | Fixed, 2,800 rpm | Adjustable, 200 to 3,000 rpm |
| Modes | Continuous (on/off switch) | Touch and continuous |
| Power | 50 W | 60 W |
| Warranty | 1 year | 2 years |

## 2. Safety

- Place the mixer on a level, non-slip surface.
- Hold tubes firmly and do not overfill them.
- Do not mix flammable or hazardous liquids unless you have assessed the risk.
- Unplug before cleaning.

## 3. Setting up

1. Check the contents against the packing list.
2. Place the mixer on a level surface and fit the cup head.
3. Connect the mains cable (230 V AC, 50 Hz).

## 4. Operation

1. Basic: switch on for continuous mixing at 2,800 rpm. Variable speed: set the speed with the dial (200 to 3,000 rpm).
2. Press the tube lightly into the cup head. In touch mode (Variable speed only) the mixer runs while the tube presses the head.
3. Mix for the time needed, then lift the tube away. Switch off when finished.
4. Do not run for more than 30 minutes continuously; rest for 10 minutes.

## 5. Cleaning and maintenance

- Wipe the head and casing with a damp cloth and mild detergent.
- Do not immerse in liquid.

## 6. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does not start | Mains cable not connected | Check the cable and socket |
| Tube does not mix well | Speed too low or tube overfilled | Increase the speed or reduce the volume |

## 7. Warranty and contact

Warranty: Basic: 1 year. Variable speed: 2 years.
