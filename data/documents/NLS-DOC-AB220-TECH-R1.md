## Purpose

This note explains where to site the AB-220, how to warm it up and how to calibrate it. It applies to both versions.

## Siting

- Use a firm bench, away from draughts, vibration, direct sunlight and air conditioning outlets.
- Operating conditions are 15 to 30 °C and 20 to 80 % humidity, non-condensing.
- Level the balance with the feet until the level indicator is centred.

## Warm-up

Leave the balance powered for 30 minutes before use or calibration, so the electronics reach a stable temperature.

## Calibration routine

1. After warm-up, press and hold the calibration key.
2. Place the 200 g test weight on the centre of the pan when prompted (the weight is not supplied).
3. Remove the weight when prompted and wait for the confirmation message.
4. Recalibrate after moving the balance or after a large temperature change.

## Drift

If readings drift, check for draughts, static charge on the sample or container, and temperature differences between the sample and the balance.

## Certified version

The calibration certificate supplied with the AB-220 Certified applies to that unit only. It does not replace regular calibration checks by the user.
