## 1. Identification

Product: nitrile examination gloves, powder-free (SKU NLS-GL-NM). Supplier: Northfield Lab Supply.

## 2. Hazards

The product is an article and is not classified as hazardous. Some people may react to nitrile; stop use if irritation occurs.

## 3. Composition

Nitrile rubber. Contains no natural rubber latex and no powder.

## 4. First aid

Skin irritation: remove the gloves, wash the skin and seek medical advice if it persists.

## 5. Handling and storage

Store cool and dry, away from direct sunlight and ozone sources.

## 6. Disposal

Dispose of as general waste, or as clinical waste if contaminated, following local rules.
