## 1. Introduction

The MC-10 Basic is a discontinued benchtop microcentrifuge with a 10-place rotor for 1.5 ml tubes and one fixed speed. It has been replaced by the MC-12 Standard.

## 2. Safety

- Place the unit on a level, stable surface.
- Never run with an unbalanced load or with the lid open.
- Unplug before cleaning or moving.

## 3. Setting up

1. Check the contents: unit, 10-place rotor, mains cable (UK plug).
2. Connect the mains cable (230 V AC, 50 Hz).
3. Fit the rotor and tighten the nut by hand.

## 4. Operation

1. Load tubes in opposite positions and balance odd numbers with a water-filled tube.
2. Close the lid and press Start. The unit runs at 12,000 rpm (10,800 ×g).
3. Wait for the rotor to stop before opening the lid.

## 5. Cleaning and maintenance

- Wipe with a damp cloth and mild detergent. Do not autoclave the rotor.

## 6. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does not start | Lid not latched | Close the lid until it clicks |
| Vibration | Unbalanced tubes | Rebalance the tubes |
