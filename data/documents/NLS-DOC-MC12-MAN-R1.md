## 1. Introduction

The MC-12 is a compact benchtop microcentrifuge for 1.5 ml and 2.0 ml tubes. It is sold in two versions: the Standard (fixed speed settings) and the Plus (variable speed, digital display and a second rotor).

Differences between the versions:

|  | Standard | Plus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed | 3 fixed settings: 4,000, 8,000, 13,000 rpm | Variable 500 to 15,000 rpm in 100 rpm steps |
| Maximum RCF | 14,200 ×g | 18,900 ×g |
| Rotors supplied | 12-place (1.5/2.0 ml tubes) | 12-place (1.5/2.0 ml tubes) and 8-place PCR strip rotor |
| Display and timer | None; run continuously or short-spin | Digital speed and timer display; 10 s to 99 min or continuous |
| Warranty | 1 year | 2 years |

## 2. Safety

- Place the unit on a level, stable surface with at least 10 cm clearance on all sides.
- Never run the centrifuge with an unbalanced load or with the lid open. The lid lock and imbalance cut-out are safety devices, not operating controls.
- Do not use tubes that are cracked, deformed or rated below the maximum RCF of your version.
- Do not spin flammable, explosive or hazardous biological material unless you have assessed the risk.
- Unplug the unit before cleaning or moving it.

## 3. Setting up

1. Remove the packaging and check the contents against the packing list: centrifuge unit, rotor(s), mains cable (UK plug) and this manual.
2. Place the unit on a level surface and connect the mains cable (230 V AC, 50 Hz).
3. Fit the rotor by pressing it onto the drive spindle until it seats, then tighten the rotor nut by hand.
4. Run the unit empty at the lowest speed for 10 seconds to confirm it runs smoothly.

## 4. Operation

1. Load an even number of tubes in opposite positions. For an odd number, balance with a tube of the same type and the same volume of water.
2. Close the lid until it latches.
3. Standard: choose one of the three speed settings, then press Start to run continuously, or hold Short spin for a brief run. Plus: set the speed and the time on the display, then press Start.
4. Wait until the rotor has stopped completely before opening the lid. Braking takes about 15 seconds.
5. Remove the tubes carefully and switch off at the mains.

## 5. Cleaning and maintenance

- Wipe the chamber and rotor with a damp cloth and mild detergent after use. Dry before closing the lid.
- Do not autoclave the rotor. Inspect it monthly for cracks or corrosion and replace it if damaged.
- Clean spills immediately; disinfect with 70 % ethanol if biological material has spilled.

## 6. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does not start | Lid not latched; mains cable not connected | Close the lid until it clicks; check the cable and socket |
| Loud vibration | Unbalanced tubes | Stop the run, check the tubes are balanced and opposite each other |
| Stops early or shows an error (Plus) | Imbalance cut-out triggered | Rebalance the load and restart |
| Rotor does not seat | Spindle dirty | Clean the spindle and rotor bore and refit |
