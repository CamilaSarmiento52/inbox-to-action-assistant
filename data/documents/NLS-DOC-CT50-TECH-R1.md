## Purpose

This note explains how to use the Northfield conical centrifuge tubes 50 ml in a centrifuge.

## Rotors

Use the tubes in fixed-angle or swing-bucket rotors with buckets or adapters designed for 50 ml conical tubes.

## Centrifugal limit

The tubes are rated to 15,000 ×g with the cap closed.

## Filling

- Fill to no more than the 50 ml graduation.
- Close the screw cap fully before centrifuging.
- Balance tubes in opposite positions, as for any centrifuge load.

## Not covered

Use in rotors or adapters not designed for 50 ml conical tubes is not covered by this note.
