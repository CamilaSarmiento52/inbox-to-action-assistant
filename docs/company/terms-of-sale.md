# Terms of sale

Version 0.1 (draft) · effective 2026-10-06 · status: current

> Fictional terms for the fictional company Northfield Lab Supply. Not legal advice.

Prices, discount tiers and delivery charges are held only in the catalogue, discount and delivery tables. These terms say how they are applied, never what they are.

## 1. Prices and currency
1.1 All prices are in pounds sterling and shown excluding VAT.
1.2 VAT is added at the rate in force, currently 20%, and shown separately on quotations and invoices.
1.3 Prices are per the selling unit in the catalogue (for example each, pack or case). A quotation names the unit.

## 2. Quotations
2.1 A quotation is an offer based on the products, versions, quantities and delivery destination stated in it.
2.2 A quotation is valid for 30 days from its date.
2.3 A quotation is not an order. An order exists only once we confirm it in writing.
2.4 A quotation shows, per line: product and version, quantity, unit price, discount (if any), and the line total. It then shows the delivery charge, VAT and the total.
2.5 If a request lacks the product, version, quantity or delivery destination, we ask for the missing detail. We may state prices that can already be established, and say what remains open.
2.6 Where a requested product is not in the catalogue, we may suggest a labelled alternative. We never substitute it without saying so. A product's absence from the catalogue does not tell us whether it is in stock.

## 3. Discounts
3.1 Quantity discounts apply only as set out in the discount table.
3.2 Where we cannot tell whether a discount applies, a person decides before the quotation is sent.
3.3 Order of calculation: line total, then discount, then delivery charge, then VAT. A free-delivery threshold is judged on the discounted goods total, ex VAT.

## 4. Orders and payment
4.1 We confirm orders in writing after a person approves them.
4.2 Approved account customers pay within 30 days of the invoice date. Other customers pay before dispatch.
4.3 Payment questions go to Finance. This assistant does not take payments.

## 5. Invoices
5.1 Invoices are issued by Finance and linked to the customer account and order.
5.2 Copies are sent only to authorised contacts of the account that owns the invoice. See the data-protection notice.

## 6. Delivery and risk
Delivery is governed by the delivery policy. Risk passes to the customer on delivery.

## 7. Returns, shortages and damage
Governed by the returns and complaints policy.

## 8. Changes
We may update these terms. A quotation is governed by the terms current on its date.
