# Returns and complaints policy

Version 0.1 (draft) · effective 2026-10-06 · status: current

> Fictional policy for the fictional company Northfield Lab Supply.

## 1. Reporting problems
| Problem | Report within | Report to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shortage, visible transit damage, wrong item | 48 hours of receipt, in writing | Customer service |
| Quality problem with a product | 14 days of receipt, in writing | Customer service |
| Convenience return of a standard catalogue item | 30 days of delivery | Customer service |

Windows are counted in calendar days from the recorded delivery date; 48 hours means 2 days.

Reports should include the order number, the product, the quantity affected, a description of the problem and, where useful, photographs and batch numbers.

## 2. Returns authorisation
2.1 Goods are not returned until we issue a written return authorisation number. Unauthorised returns may be refused.
2.2 Returned items must be unused, in original packaging, and, for anything that has been in contact with biological or chemical material, accompanied by a signed decontamination declaration. We do not accept contaminated items.
2.3 Convenience returns may carry a restocking fee, decided by a person when the return is authorised; the assistant never states an amount. Not accepted: opened sterile items, made-to-order items, and items past the return window.

## 3. Remedies
Possible remedies are replacement, refund, credit note, or no action. Which applies depends on the facts. Examples: replacement for shipped-short or damaged items, credit or refund where a replacement is not possible.

## 4. Who decides
4.1 The assistant gathers information and proposes a remedy from this policy. It never approves, promises or records a remedy as agreed.
4.2 Technical / quality review decides quality complaints and returns. Logistics decides on shortages, partial deliveries, wrong items and goods damaged in transit, with Customer service.
4.3 A proposed remedy is always shown as proposed until a person approves it.

## 5. Information needed to assess a complaint
1. The order number and the customer.
2. The product and version, with the quantity affected.
3. What went wrong, and when it was noticed.
4. Evidence where available (photographs, batch or lot number).
5. What the customer is asking for.
The assistant asks for whatever is missing.

## 6. Mixed requests
If a complaint arrives with a quotation request, both parts are handled and answered together once the complaint review is complete.
