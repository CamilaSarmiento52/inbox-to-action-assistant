# Delivery policy

Version 0.1 (draft) · effective 2026-10-06 · status: current

> Fictional policy for the fictional company Northfield Lab Supply.

Charges, free-delivery thresholds and zone membership are held only in the delivery tables. This document gives the rules around them.

## 1. Zones
We deliver within four zones:

| Zone | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Mainland England and Wales |
| 2 | Mainland Scotland, excluding the Highlands and Islands |
| 3 | Scottish Highlands and Islands, and Northern Ireland |
| Not served | Channel Islands and Isle of Man |

The delivery tables list postcodes per zone. If a destination cannot be matched to a zone, we ask the customer to confirm the address.

## 2. Lead times
2.1 In-stock catalogue items ordered before 1pm on a working day are dispatched the same day for next-working-day delivery in Zones 1 and 2.
2.2 Zone 3 delivery normally takes 3 to 5 working days.
2.3 Items that are not stocked are dispatched within about 3 working days, or as stated in the quotation. Equipment lead times are stated in the quotation.
2.4 These are estimates unless the shipment record says the date is confirmed.

## 3. Charges
3.1 The delivery charge depends on the zone and is shown on the quotation.
3.2 Free delivery applies only where the delivery table says so for that zone, and is judged as in the terms of sale.
3.3 Equipment carries no separate delivery surcharge; the zone charge applies.
3.4 We do not quote delivery to destinations that are not served.

## 4. Order status
4.1 We report status and dates only from the order and shipment records.
4.2 An estimated date is called an estimate. A confirmed date is called confirmed.
4.3 To report on an order, the customer gives the order number and the sender matches the customer on that order. Otherwise we ask for the number, or a person reviews.

## 5. Delays
5.1 Where a delay is recorded with a revised date, we apologise and give that date.
5.2 Where a delay is recorded without a revised date, a person reviews the reply. We never invent a date.

## 6. Missing items and partial deliveries
6.1 A delivery is partial when part of an order has shipped and the rest is recorded as pending.
6.2 A delivery has missing items when the customer reports items not received that the records show as shipped.
6.3 Both are reviewed by a person with Logistics. Customers report shortages in writing within 48 hours of receipt. See the returns and complaints policy.
