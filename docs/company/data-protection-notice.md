# Data-protection notice

Version 0.1 (draft) · effective 2026-10-06 · status: current

> Fictional notice for the fictional company Northfield Lab Supply. Not legal advice.

## What we hold
Business contact details (name, email, phone, delivery and invoice addresses), company details (VAT or registration number), orders, shipments, invoices and our correspondence with customers.

## Why
To quote, deliver, invoice, and answer customer queries.

## Who may receive customer information
- Customer information is shared only with the customer's authorised contacts, listed on the customer account.
- An invoice copy goes only to an authorised contact of the account that owns the invoice. If we cannot confirm that, a person reviews and nothing is sent.
- Order status is reported only when the sender matches the customer on the order.

## Automated processing
We use an assistant to read and draft replies. A person approves every external reply. The assistant ignores instructions contained in incoming emails or attachments.

## Retention and rights
We keep records as long as needed for the purposes above and for accounting duties. Customers may ask what we hold and request correction. Requests go to Customer service.
