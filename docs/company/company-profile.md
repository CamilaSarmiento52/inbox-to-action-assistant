# Northfield Lab Supply: company profile

Version 0.1 (draft) · effective 2026-10-06 · status: current

> Northfield Lab Supply is a fictional company created for a portfolio project. It does not exist and none of its prices, policies or contacts are real.

## Who we are

Northfield Lab Supply is a UK distributor of laboratory consumables and small laboratory equipment. We supply schools, universities, clinical and research laboratories, and small businesses in the United Kingdom.

## What we sell

- Laboratory consumables such as pipette tips, centrifuge tubes, gloves and sample containers.
- Small equipment such as benchtop centrifuges, balances and heating blocks. Some equipment is offered in more than one version with different inclusions.

Current products, versions, pack sizes and prices are held only in the product catalogue. This document never states a price.

## Where we operate

We serve customers in the United Kingdom and price in pounds sterling (GBP). We do not currently serve the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man. See the delivery policy.

## Mission and values

- **Mission:** make it easy for laboratories to get the right supplies, on time, with clear information.
- **Accuracy:** we quote and report only what our records support.
- **Clarity:** we say what we know, what we do not, and what happens next.
- **Care:** a person reviews what we send to customers.

## Departments

| Department | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Sales | Quotations, new customer accounts |
| Customer service | General enquiries, product documents, anything unclear |
| Logistics | Orders, shipments, delivery issues |
| Finance | Invoices, payment questions |
| Technical / quality review | Technical questions, quality complaints, returns |

All customers contact us through one shared customer-service inbox: customerservice@northfieldlabsupply.example. This is also the contact address printed on every product document.

## How we write to customers

- English, polite and plain. Short paragraphs, no jargon beyond product names.
- Address the customer by name where known. Sign off with the team name, not an individual.
- Never promise what the records do not support. An estimated date is described as an estimate.
- Every external reply is approved by a person before it is sent. See the communication and escalation policy.
- Replies state that Northfield Lab Supply is fictional only where a document or demo requires it.
